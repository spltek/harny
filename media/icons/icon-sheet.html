Icon sheet

All of them are registered in src/chat/icons.ts and inlined at build time, so what you see here is what the panel renders. A tool call wears its own mark: toolIcon("StrReplace") → strreplace.svg, every MCP tool shares the plug, and an unknown name keeps the wrench.

Two weights, on purpose. Every mark the TRANSCRIPT draws is stroke-width 1.5 on the same 24×24 grid — about 0.8 px once drawn at 13 px. The chrome runs at 2: a 1.5 line at 10–11 px is 0.7 px and disappears. sparkles is the one 1.8, only chevron-down keeps a 2.5 (a chevron has no joins to hold it together), and five marks are SOLID with no stroke at all (gear, play, skill, stop, parallel) — a filled silhouette reads heavier than any line at the same size. harny.svg is the sixth silhouette AND the only file carrying its own PAINT, a literal #d08856 instead of currentColor: it is a picture, not a glyph, and that tan is the brand's ORIGINAL colour (the old gradient's midpoint, flattened) — deliberately NOT the bubble's user-msg, which stays the burnt accent (see the README). The brand ships in TWO frames, and that is the only reason there are two files of one drawing: harny.svg is not on the shared 24x24 canvas — the whole logo (bubble + answering caret) is 15:11, and the panel gives it a box of exactly that shape — while harny-app.svg is the same drawing INSET in the square the activity bar paints into. Where one drawing has to serve two weights, the call site asks for the one it needs (icon("trash", 13, 'stroke-width="2"'), used twice in the session list) instead of keeping a second copy of the file.
